# Installation Guide for the Ordbøkene App

You can use this app both online and offline. The app works on computers, mobile phones and tablets, and can be used with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Safari on Windows, Android, macOS and iOS.

Below you will find instructions on how to download the Ordbøkene app. First, there is a brief set of instructions that apply across devices and operating systems, followed by more detailed instructions for each operating system.

### General instructions for all operating systems

- Connect your device to the internet.
- Go to [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) to download the app and its content.
- Keep the installed app open while the content is being downloaded.
- On mobile phones and tablets, keep the screen on until the download is complete.

### Windows: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Select the installation icon on the right-hand side of the header.

![The installation icon on the right-hand side of the header in Chrome or Edge on Windows.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-win-2.png)

3. An installation window will appear in your browser. Select **Install**.

    Google Chrome:

    ![The installation window in Google Chrome, showing the Install button.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-win-3.png)

    Microsoft Edge:
   
    ![The installation window in Microsoft Edge, showing the Install button.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-win-3-edge.png)

4. Open the installed app if it does not open automatically.
5. Keep the app open while it downloads and prepares the dictionary entries. This may take a few minutes, depending on your internet connection and device.

    ![The installed app downloading and preparing the dictionary entries on Windows.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-win-5.png)

6. Follow the progress indicator at the top of the app and wait until the dictionaries are ready for offline use.

The app is available from the Windows Start menu. You can also pin it to the taskbar for easier access.

### Android: Google Chrome

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Chrome.
2. Tap the install icon on the right-hand side of the header.

    ![The installation icon on the right-hand side of the Ordbøkene header in Chrome on Android.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-andr-2.png)

3. Tap **Install** in the browser dialog.

    ![The installation dialog in Chrome on Android, with an Install button.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-andr-3.png)

4. Once the installation is complete, open the Ordbøkene app from the Home Screen or app list.
5. Keep the app in the foreground and the screen on while the dictionary entries are being downloaded and prepared.

    ![The installed app downloading and preparing the dictionary entries on Android.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-andr-5.png)

6. Follow the progress indicator at the top of the app and wait until the dictionaries are ready for offline use.

Android may pause the download if the screen locks or you move the app to the background. If this happens, open the app again to resume the download.

### macOS: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Select the installation icon on the right-hand side of the header.
3. Select **Install** in the installation window.
4. Open the Ordbøkene app from the Dock, Launchpad, Spotlight or Applications folder.
5. Keep the app open while it downloads and prepares the dictionary entries.
6. Wait until the dictionaries are ready for offline use.

### macOS: Safari

Web apps in Safari require macOS Sonoma 14 or later.

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Safari.
2. Select the **Share** button in the Safari toolbar and choose **Add to Dock**. Alternatively, select **File > Add to Dock** from the menu bar.

    ![The Share button in the Safari toolbar with the Add to Dock option on macOS.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-mac-saf-2.png)

3. Check the app information and select **Add**.

    ![The app information dialogue in Safari, showing the Add button.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-mac-saf-3.png)

4. Open the Ordbøkene app from the Dock, Spotlight or Applications folder.
5. Keep the app open while it downloades and prepares the dictionary entries.

    ![The installed app downloading and preparing the dictionary entries on macOS.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-mac-saf-5.png)

6. Wait until the dictionaries are ready for offline use.


### iPhone or iPad: Safari

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Safari.
2. Tap the button with three dots in the bottom-right corner of Safari.

    ![The three-dot button at the bottom right of Safari on iPhone or iPad.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-ios-2.png)

3. Select **Share**.

    ![The Share option in the Safari menu on iPhone or iPad.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-ios-3.png)

4. Scroll through the available actions and select **Add to Home Screen**.

    ![The Add to Home Screen action in the Share sheet on iPhone or iPad.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-ios-4.png)
    ![The app details screen with an Add button on iPhone or iPad.](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-ios-4.1.png)

5. Check the app information and tap **Add**.
6. Open the Ordbøkene app from the Home Screen.
7. Keep the app in the foreground and the screen on while the app downloads and prepares the dictionary entries. This may take a few minutes, depending on your internet connection and device.
8. Follow the progress indicator at the top of the app and wait until the dictionaries are ready for offline use.

### Check that the app works offline

1. Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data, or enable Airplane Mode.
2. Open the installed app, enter a word in the search field and search.
