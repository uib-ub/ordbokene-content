# Ordbøkene offline app installation guide

Installing the app and downloading the offline dictionaries are two separate steps. You must complete both steps before using the app without an internet connection.

### Before you begin

- Connect your device to the internet.
- Go to [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no).
- Keep the installed app open while the dictionaries are being prepared.
- On a phone or tablet, keep the screen awake until the download is complete.

### Windows: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Select the install icon on the right-hand side of the Ordbøkene header.
![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-win-2.png)
3. Your browser will display an installation dialog. Select **Install**.

    Google Chrome:

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-win-3.png)

    Microsoft Edge:
   
    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-win-3-edge.png)


4. Open the installed app if it does not open automatically.
5. Keep the app open while it downloads and prepares the dictionary articles. This may take a few minutes, depending on your internet connection and device.
    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-win-5.png)
6. Follow the progress at the top of the app and wait until the offline dictionaries are ready.

The app is available from the Windows Start menu. You can also pin it to the taskbar for easier access.


### macOS: Google Chrome

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Chrome.
2. Select the install icon on the right-hand side of the Ordbøkene header. You can also use the install icon in Chrome's address bar or choose the option to install the page as an app from the Chrome menu.
3. Select **Install** in the installation dialog.
4. Open Ordbøkene from the Dock, Launchpad, Spotlight or the Applications folder.
5. Keep the app open while the dictionary articles are downloaded and prepared.
6. Wait until the offline dictionaries are ready.

### macOS: Safari

Safari web apps require macOS Sonoma 14 or later.

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Safari.
2. Select the **Share** button in the Safari toolbar and choose **Add to Dock**. You can also choose **File > Add to Dock** from the menu bar.

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-mac-saf-2.png)

3. Confirm the app details and select **Add**.

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-mac-saf-3.png)


4. Open Ordbøkene from the Dock, Spotlight or the Applications folder.
5. Keep the app open while the dictionary articles are downloaded and prepared.

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-mac-saf-5.png)

6. Wait until the offline dictionaries are ready.

### Android: Google Chrome

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Chrome.
2. Tap the install icon on the right-hand side of the Ordbøkene header.

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-andr-2.png)
3. Tap **Install** in the browser dialog.

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-andr-3.png)


4. When installation is complete, open Ordbøkene from the Home Screen or app list.
5. Keep the app in the foreground and keep the screen awake while the dictionary articles are downloaded and prepared.

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-andr-5.png)

6. Follow the progress at the top of the app and wait until the offline dictionaries are ready.

Android may pause the download when the screen is locked or the app is moved to the background. Reopen the app to continue if this happens.

### iPhone or iPad: Safari

1. Open [pwa.ordbokene.no](https://pwa.ordbokene.no) in Safari.
2. Tap the three-dot button at the bottom right of Safari.

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-ios-2.png)

3. Select **Share**.

    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-ios-3.png)


4. Scroll through the available actions and select **Add to Home Screen**.
    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-ios-4.png)
    ![alt text](/content-images/pwa/pwa-eng-ios-4.1.png)
5. Confirm the app details and tap **Add**.
6. Open Ordbøkene from the Home Screen.
7. Keep the app in the foreground and keep the screen awake while the dictionary articles are downloaded and prepared. This may take a few minutes, depending on your internet connection and device.
8. Follow the progress at the top of the app and wait until the offline dictionaries are ready.

### Check that the app works offline

1. Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data, or enable airplane mode.
2. Open the installed app and perform a dictionary search.
